06 / Reference

Coverage & methods

{{COVERAGE}}.

The requested window is split into equal calendar periods. Activities use their local start date; daily readings use Garmin’s calendar date. Training totals require a complete, dated activity search. Missing values remain missing; a day with no workouts is zero only when the activity search is complete. Daily comparisons require readings for every day in both periods.

Outdoor pace is total recorded running time divided by total distance. Treadmill sessions are excluded from outdoor comparisons. The distance check flags running records under 100 m over at least 10 minutes. This period comparison describes recent workload and recovery observations; fitness progression needs comparable efforts over a longer period.

The seven-hour sleep line uses CDC adult sleep guidance. HRV interpretation follows Garmin’s personal-baseline explanation. See Garmin’s training status documentation for its longer-term measures.
